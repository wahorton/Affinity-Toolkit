FBT Exempt Benefits
  • Minor benefits <$300 taxable value (infrequent and irregular)
  • Work-related items: laptops, phones, tools of trade (1 per year)
  • In-house gym / recreation facilities
  • Emergency assistance / medical treatment
  • Relocation costs (exempt amounts)
  • Car parking (below threshold, or remote area)
FBT year runs 1 April to 31 March. The FBT rate and gross-up rates are for 2024–25 and 2025–26. Figures shown are indicative only. FBT involves complex provisions including exempt benefits, otherwise deductible rule, employee contributions and the reportable fringe benefits amount. Always verify against ATO FBT publications.
