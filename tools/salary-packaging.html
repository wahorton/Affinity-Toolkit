Key Notes
Reportable amounts: RFBA and RESC are added back for means-testing purposes (Medicare levy surcharge, HELP repayment, government benefits, child support). Salary packaging doesn't always improve overall outcomes for lower-income earners.
Concessional cap: Total concessional contributions (SG + salary sacrifice) must not exceed $30,000 p.a. (2024–25) or $30,000 p.a. (2025–26). Excess contributions are included in assessable income with a 15% offset.
This calculator provides an indicative comparison only. It applies resident individual tax rates and does not account for HELP repayment, Medicare Levy Surcharge, LMITO, or state-specific taxes. FBT costs shown assume the employer passes them on. Always review packaging arrangements with reference to the employee's full income and benefit position.
