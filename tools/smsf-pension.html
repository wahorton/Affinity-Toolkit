Key Pension Rules
Failure to meet minimum pension: The pension is taken to have never been a pension. All earnings for the year are taxable at 15% (not exempt). The ATO may grant Commissioner's discretion in limited circumstances.
  • Minimum must be met by 30 June each year (or the pension is deemed to have failed).
  • For new pensions commenced mid-year, the minimum is pro-rated based on days remaining in the financial year ÷ 365.
  • Account balance for minimum calculation is the balance at 1 July (or at commencement for new pensions).
  • Market-linked pensions (MLPs) have different rules — not calculated here.
  • Age as at 1 July of the income year is used to determine the drawdown rate.
This tool calculates minimum pension drawdown for account-based pensions under ITAR 1997 Schedule 7. It does not apply to defined benefit pensions, transition-to-retirement pensions (different rules), or market-linked pensions. Always verify balances and ages before advising clients.
