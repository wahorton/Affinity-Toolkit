Important Notes
CGT event A1 (disposal of asset) is assumed. Other CGT events (B1, C2, D1, E5, E6, K6, etc.) require separate analysis.

Small business concessions (15-year exemption, 50% active asset reduction, retirement exemption, rollover) are NOT applied here — they may significantly reduce or eliminate CGT for eligible small business owners.

Main residence exemption is NOT applied here. A full or partial exemption may apply.
This tool provides an indicative CGT calculation only. It does not account for the main residence exemption, small business CGT concessions, the indexation method (for assets acquired before 21 Sept 1999), CGT rollovers, or complex trust distribution scenarios. Always obtain specific advice for individual client situations.
